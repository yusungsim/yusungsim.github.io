I am a Ph.D. student in the School of Computing at KAIST, Daejeon, Republic of Korea, in the Programming Language Research Group (PLRG), advised by Professor Sukyoung Ryu. My research interests lie in programming languages and their formal specifications, with a particular focus on type systems.
